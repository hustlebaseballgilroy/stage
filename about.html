About Hustle

Established in 2024, Hustle Baseball is a youth travel baseball nonprofit in Gilroy, California — dedicated to developing young athletes on and off the field, and offering a competitive pathway beyond traditional little league.

01

Our Vision

To be the leading youth baseball organization that inspires excellence through sportsmanship, fosters personal and athletic growth, and celebrates the hustle and determination of every player. We envision a vibrant community where young athletes thrive, families connect, and the values of teamwork and respect shape future leaders both on and off the field.

02

Our Mission

To foster a love for youth baseball by cultivating a culture of sportsmanship, relentless hustle, and personal growth. We are committed to building a strong sense of community, where players, families, and supporters come together to inspire the next generation to excel both on and off the field.

03

Our Purpose

To empower young athletes through the game of baseball, teaching the values of sportsmanship, growth, and hustle. We aim to build a supportive community that nurtures character, encourages personal development, and instills a lifelong love for the sport.

The Program

More Than a Team

Hustle Baseball is a community dedicated to developing young athletes both on and off the field. Based in Gilroy, we compete in tournaments throughout the region, giving our players exposure to different levels of competition.

Our commitment to player development has made us one of the emerging forces in youth travel baseball in the Gilroy area.

What we focus on

  • Building strong fundamental baseball skills
  • Developing leadership and teamwork
  • Creating a positive and supportive environment
  • Preparing players for high school and beyond
  • Competing in local travel baseball tournaments
  • Providing opportunities beyond local little league

Nic Amanno11U · Head Coach

For Coach Nic, baseball is far more than just a game; it's a powerful and practical teacher of life. He believes the sport builds character, teaching loyalty, commitment, and hard work in a way that prepares young players for success in any career. His coaching philosophy is centered on making sure players have fun and develop a true love for the game while getting better each day.

Read full bio

He focuses on a "building block" approach to learning, especially with hitting, believing that positive self-talk and self-belief are the most important qualities for a young player to develop.

Coach Nic's journey in baseball started simply, with his dad signing him up for tee-ball. What captivated him from the start was the blend of individual skill and teamwork the sport demands. As a player, he was an All-American relief pitcher for Cuesta Community College, where he helped lead his team to the state final four. He then earned a full scholarship to pitch for Chico State, helping his team reach the NCAA Division II World Series and being named National Pitcher of the Week twice. While those achievements are a source of pride, his greatest joy now comes from coaching — his proudest moment is simply hearing a player say they liked his coaching and felt they improved because of it.

His connection to the team is personal: he is the proud dad of his son, Nolan. He hopes to be a positive influence in the lives of young players and their families and to be part of the Gilroy baseball community for many years to come. Away from the ballpark you'll find him with his family, BBQing, or doing yard work — though, as he says, "I would be at the ballpark all day every day if I could."

DS
Dan Stilts11U · Coach

Coach Dan's journey with baseball began at a young age, playing every day with friends in his neighborhood. That love for the game led him to join a team in the fourth grade, where he gravitated toward pitching, second base, and first base.

Read full bio

His coaching story started in tee-ball, helping out with his oldest son Eli's team. After seeing some negative experiences with other coaches, Coach Dan was inspired to step up and provide a more positive environment. He found his calling coaching his youngest son Evan's 8U Little League team, where he loved helping kids grow and improve while having fun.

Coach Dan's philosophy is centered on being a positive role model. He believes the most crucial qualities for a young player are confidence and resilience. He teaches kids to believe in themselves, to embrace failure as part of the process, and to understand that sportsmanship is just as important as the final score.

A proud member of the Gilroy baseball community for over five years, Coach Dan is honored to coach his son Evan and to be part of his son Eli's baseball journey with Hustle. Away from the field he enjoys cooking, grilling, building custom pieces in his wood and metal shop, and relaxing on the lake with his family.

Coach Mike brings a wonderful mix of passion, discipline, knowledge, and excitement to his instruction of baseball. A lifelong baseball fan, he thrives on sharing that enthusiasm with the next generation of players.

Known for his ability to connect effortlessly with kids, Coach Mike creates a supportive and structured environment where young athletes feel encouraged to learn, grow, and love the game.

With a strong focus on proper mechanics and baseball fundamentals, he makes sure each player builds a solid foundation of skills. His disciplined coaching style helps players develop not just as athletes, but as teammates who understand the value of hard work, accountability, and sportsmanship.

Above all, Coach Mike is deeply committed to the personal and athletic development of every player he works with. He takes pride in watching his players improve over time and finds genuine joy in helping them reach their potential — on and off the field.
