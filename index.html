Who We Are

More Than a Team

Established in 2024, Hustle Baseball is a competitive youth travel baseball organization based in Gilroy, California — a pathway for youth baseball development beyond traditional little league programs.

As Gilroy's premier travel baseball team, we give young athletes the chance to compete at a higher level while learning lessons that last — serving families in Gilroy and the surrounding areas.

01

Our Vision

To be the leading youth baseball organization that inspires excellence through sportsmanship and fosters personal and athletic growth.

02

Our Mission

To foster a love for youth baseball by cultivating a culture of sportsmanship, relentless hustle, and personal growth — while building community among players, families, and supporters.

03

Our Purpose

To empower young athletes through the game of baseball, teaching the values of sportsmanship, growth, and hustle while developing character and a lifelong passion for the sport.

How is travel ball different from little league?

Hustle teams compete in local tournaments close to home, so there's no long-distance travel. The program is a development pathway beyond traditional little league, built around fundamentals, sportsmanship, and hustle.

Where do the teams play tournaments?

We play local tournaments run by CCYB, USSSA and NCTB, at venues like Manzanita Fields in Prunedale, Big League Dreams in Manteca and Twin Creeks in Sunnyvale. See Tournament Sites for addresses and tips.

How do families keep up with the schedule?

Games, practices and tournaments are kept in GameChanger and show up on our Schedule page. You can also subscribe to your team's calendar so new events appear on your phone automatically.

Are donations or player fees tax-deductible?

Hustle Baseball is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (Tax ID 30-1433334), so donations may be tax-deductible. Player and registration fees generally are not, because they pay for your own player's participation. Volunteers may be able to deduct out-of-pocket costs such as mileage. Please check with your tax advisor.

How can my business sponsor the team?

Sponsorships help cover uniforms, tournament fees, and equipment. See the sponsorship levels or email info@hustlebaseballgilroy.org.
